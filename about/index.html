About

Hi, I'm Tavor.

It's a name I share with a mountain, a village, a winery, a European tranquilizer, and only about 260 other women in Israel.

I'm drawn to building products for experts and making them work for the people who actually have to use them. I find what the product really needs and shape it into tools people navigate with confidence.

My foundation is visual design and art direction. For six years I've been a product designer, and I was Coro's first in-house one. Cybersecurity is where I've gone deepest, alongside public-sector systems and medical work.

How I work

Design-true vibe coding

Today I work in Claude Code, and I know how to get good results from it, so a design moves from the design file into the product's code the way I designed it. I design knowing the end result is code: working with front-end developers taught me what a design needs in order to be built, and I carry that into every screen.

Bringing UX into the organization

In places where no one has worked with a designer before, I start by listening: how the work is done today, and where it gets stuck. I design alongside the people who will use the system, so UX becomes part of how the team works and not a step added at the end.

Turning complexity into clarity

Tangled workflows and unfamiliar screens are where I do my best work. I cut through the noise, keep one clear next step on every screen, and say in plain words what will happen before anyone commits.
